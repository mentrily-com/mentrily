## 2024-07-22 - Screen Reader Attributes for Custom Dropdowns\n**Learning:** Custom dropdown toggles (like hamburger menus or profile avatars) often rely solely on `aria-label`, missing crucial state information (`aria-expanded`) and structural relationship (`aria-haspopup="menu"`) required by screen readers.\n**Action:** Always verify that interactive custom menus use both `aria-expanded={isOpen}` and `aria-haspopup="menu"` to provide accurate state announcements.
