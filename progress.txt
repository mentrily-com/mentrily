## US-002: Enforce Strict Tenant Scoping on All Organization-Owned Entity Queries
- Updated `CourseService.assertTenantOrOwnerAccess` and `CourseService.assertCourseAccess` to reject null users and prevent creator bypass when active tenant (`user.orgId`) does not match `course.orgId`.
- Updated `ExamService.assertTenantOrOwnerAccess`, `ExamService.canAccessPublicExamResource`, and `ExamService.assertExamOrgAccess` to enforce org scoping, reject null users, and reject cross-tenant access with `403 Forbidden` / `404 Not Found`.
- Updated `TeacherAnnouncementsService.assertAnnouncementAccess` and `TeacherAnnouncementsService.getAnnouncements` to enforce strict `orgId` filtering for teachers and admins, and reject cross-tenant mutations with `403 Forbidden`.
- Updated `StudentAnnouncementsService` (`getAnnouncements`, `getUnreadAnnouncementCount`, `markAnnouncementRead`) and `StudentController` to support tenant-scoped queries and reject cross-tenant read marks with `404 Not Found`.
- Updated `CertificateService` (`listCertificates`, `getCertificateForUser`, `getTemplate`, `updateTemplate`, `deleteTemplate`, `generateCertificate`) to enforce `orgId` scoping on certificates and templates.
- Updated `TeacherService.checkAccess` to explicitly reject cross-tenant access when `resource.orgId && user.orgId && resource.orgId !== user.orgId`.
- Added comprehensive unit tests in `course.service.spec.ts`, `exam.service.spec.ts`, `teacher-announcements.service.spec.ts`, `student-announcements.service.spec.ts`, `teacher.service.spec.ts`, and created `certificate.service.spec.ts` (39/39 test suites passed, 176 tests).
